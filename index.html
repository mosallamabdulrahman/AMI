Who We Are

The Arab Institute of Management is an official entity that aims to serve the people of the Arab world in the field of management, focusing on the application of administrative practices that are suitable for the nature and culture of our region.
Learn More

Vision

To be the leading entity that represents the values and authenticity of Arab thought in the field of management and projects, and to be the bridge that connects the latest global methodologies with the needs of the Arab labor market.
Learn More

Mission

To deliver high value professionally in project and business management, by leveraging the best global practices and adapting them to fit our unique environment and culture
